# Ali Tech style guide

Edit this file to change the look of every video. Claude reads it before each edit.

## Format
- Reels / TikTok / Shorts: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- Length: 30-60 s, decided by the content. Never pad.

## Brand
- Colours: from `~/motion-director/profile.json` (near-black background, warm cream text, lime accent). Exact hex values live in the brand's `brand.json`.
- Logo: the original logo file when available (not the Instagram avatar).

## Typography
- Font: Thmanyah (Sans for captions and body, Serif Display for big titles if installed).
- Captions: centred, 2-3 words per line, cream text with a dark outline, the spoken word highlighted in lime, placed at 70% of the height (clear of the TikTok/Instagram UI).
- Titles and graphics text: right-aligned, enter from the right.
- Technical terms stay in English inside Arabic lines (Windows 11, GPU, RAM).

## Editing
- Open on my face for the hook. Keep my face for opinions and the call to action.
- B-roll cutaways 2-3 s, steady part of the clip, their sound muted.
- Jump cuts between sentences are fine; alternate a light punch-in (1.1x) on them.
- Transitions: short fades on cutaways. Special transitions (glitch, whip, zoom) only when I ask.
- Footage shot in Log gets a grade so clips and face match. Use the camera LUT when available.

## Graphics
- Few and purposeful: numbers, app or product names, lists.
- Entry from the right, lists build right to left, progress bars fill right to left.
- Keep graphics out of the caption band (62-80% of the height).

## Sound
- Voice normalised to -14 LUFS. No music unless I ask (I add trending audio in the app).
